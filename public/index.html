❓ How to Play

🎮 The Game

Future Hide & Seek is hide-and-seek in a neon future city! One player is the Seeker 🔍, everyone else is a Hider 🙈. Pass the device around — hiders choose secret spots, then the Seeker tries to find them.

👣 Game Flow

  1. Each Hider secretly taps one hiding spot, then taps Ready — Pass to the Next Player.
  2. Hiding spots stay secret — never peek at the screen!
  3. The Seeker taps spots to search. Each Hider gets 3 guesses (4 on Beginner).
  4. The round ends when every Hider is found or escapes.
  5. Everyone takes a turn as Seeker. Highest total score = Champion! 🏆

⚡ Power-Ups

Each round, players get one power-up (Seekers get Seeker power-ups, Hiders get Hider power-ups). One use per round!

📡
Scanner Pulse (Seeker) — highlights 3 spots; one hides the target.
🤖
Robot Clue (Seeker) — a robot gives you a clue.
➕
Extra Guess (Seeker) — one more guess for this Hider.
✨
Hologram Decoy (Hider) — a fake shimmer appears elsewhere.
🔕
Silent Mode (Hider) — no shimmer hint on your spot.
🚇
Secret Tunnel (Hider) — moves you to a connected spot before the first guess.

🛡️ Safety & Privacy

No ads, no chat, no links, no purchases, no personal data. Only nicknames are used, and everything stays on your device. Perfect for classrooms and families.
